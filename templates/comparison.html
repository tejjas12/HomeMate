Model Score Overview

R² score shows the proportion of target-price variation explained by each model.

How to interpret these results

MAE and RMSE: lower values generally indicate smaller prediction errors. R²: a higher score indicates that the model explains more variation in property prices. Always compare models on the same test dataset. Actual accuracy may vary with the selected property and dataset.
